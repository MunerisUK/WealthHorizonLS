# Reporting a security problem

If you find a security problem in WealthHorizon, please email
**info@muneris.co.uk** with "Security" in the subject line. Please do not open
a public issue, and do not include your own financial data.

Tell me:

- the build number (shown in the app)
- what you did, and what happened
- whether you think it could affect other installs

I will reply to confirm I have it, and tell you what I plan to do about it.
